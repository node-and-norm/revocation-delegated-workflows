Node and Norm Temporal checker design proposal
Version 0.3 | 5 November 2026 | Proposed decisions for review
Recommendation and status
Adopt a phase-specific, artifact-only checker design that distinguishes what happened, what the caller could support, and whether the instrument was ready for measurement. Choose precise cooperative and historical-effect controls now. Learn exact native outcomes and suitable time limits during separately authorized qualification, then freeze the complete instrument before collecting fresh measured cases.
This proposal develops the unresolved decisions in PR 2. It is not a frozen protocol, implementation, executed test or experimental result. It changes no repository, historical evidence or Temporal guarantee. Native qualification remains blocked in the preserved record. No prior denial or execution restriction is lifted by this document.
The checker reads saved evidence and never drives the Worker, bridge, gateway or controller. It independently grades raw records rather than trusting runner success flags, exit codes, filenames or the proposed envelope. Hashes bind bytes, not authenticity. The evidence model assumes a reviewed, trusted fixture and does not claim adversarial producer attestation.
Proposed choices
C1 becomes a zero-execute cooperative control in both native modes. Its zero attempts and zero effects are expected outcomes, not conditions for hiding an adverse run.
C4 becomes a cancellation-only historical-effect control in both modes. Exactly one execute precedes cancellation invocation; no further execute is planned. Preserve the same committed effect through the later observation. No revocation is introduced.
K1 and K2 are explicitly sink-only controls. Native identifiers and lifecycle fields are inapplicable, not invented.
Separate held-Workflow primary evidence, postrelease evidence and cleanup evidence. Every assertion identifies its phase and evidence boundary.
Capture uses passive quiescence and complete accounting. It must not block a pending execute and then credit cancellation for the resulting absence.
Freeze schemas, exact view-by-phase allowlists, assertion opportunities, inclusion rules and cleanup criteria. Always-unresolved output receives no conclusive-coverage credit.
Keep actual native tuples and numerical deadlines unresolved until permitted qualification supplies evidence. Qualification and measurement remain separate.
Changes from version 0.2
The v0.2 result dimensions, sink accounting and receipt-backed fence distinction remain. This proposal resolves design choices for D02 through D07 provisionally, without claiming runtime qualification. D01 remains empirical. It also makes explicit a necessary refinement to A02, A03 and the earlier lifecycle obligations: schedule/configuration errors, unexpected system outcomes and evidence defects are different categories. An adverse outcome after a correctly applied schedule is retained for analysis. Source links and decision status appear at the end.
Cases and causal schedules
Every attempted case is registered before execution with an immutable case identity, source/runtime pins, selected schedule, applicable evidence requirements and expected outcomes. The six-cell measured plan remains M1 through M4 and K1/K2. C1 and C4 are qualification families. Running each of those controls in two modes creates four qualification instances, not additional measured cells. No sample size or reliability inference is implied.
Common native setup
Use an ordinary Activity with explicit TRY_CANCEL or WAIT_CANCELLATION_COMPLETED, maximumAttempts equal to 1, and declared finite timeouts. Pin actual effective options and runtime versions. The Activity runs outside the non-cancellable Workflow observation hold. Record Workflow/run identity, Activity identity, scheduled/start event linkage and actual attempt. Reject unknown behavior labels before dispatch. An observed unintended retry is retained as an adverse conformance outcome; a misconfigured retry policy is a setup defect.
Establish valid authorization under the unchanged fixture gateway before any protected attempt. Correlate the actor, grant, admission ticket, scope, root and epoch. Preserve the fixed-root, fixed-epoch and single-outstanding-operation assumptions. The local bridge request identity is correlation, not authentication and not an identity echoed by the historical gateway.
Measured schedules
M1 and M2: start the Activity; issue cancellation; record actual Activity cancellation observation; release one controlled continuation; observe release receipt and dispatch. M1 uses TRY_CANCEL; M2 uses WAIT_CANCELLATION_COMPLETED. No fence is planned. Expected sink outcome: one committed execute receipt and one matching effect.
M3 and M4: use the corresponding native mode and continuation schedule. After cancellation is observed, commit revocation and obtain its linked receipt. Only then release the continuation and allow its one planned execute. Expected sink outcome: one execute receipt, exactly one denied result with reason revoked, and zero effects. Invalid-ticket, wrong-actor or invalid-grant denial does not earn fence-protection credit.
K1: valid live authorization, one planned sink execute, no native Workflow or Activity, no cancellation and no revocation. Expected: one execute receipt and one matching effect.
K2: valid authorization and admission first; commit revocation; receive its linked receipt; perform one planned sink execute. No native lifecycle or cancellation. Expected: one execute receipt, one revoked denial and zero effects. K1/K2 have new control identities and evidence; earlier Q2 records cannot be relabeled as measured controls.
Treatment and outcome separation
The checker records the planned schedule, actual issued commands, receiver observations and actual outcomes separately. A missing required controller command or wrong configuration is a schedule/setup defect. Failure of a correctly addressed cancellation to be observed, early native closure, unexpected retry or unexpected sink effect is an observed deviation or unresolved delivery, not automatically an exclusion. An analysis requiring a missing causal observation may be unresolved, while sink facts remain usable. The fixed attempted-case record never disappears.
Cooperative and historical effect controls
C1 cooperative cancellation
Instantiate C1 once per native mode during qualification. Complete valid authorization setup and establish that the Activity has started. The cooperative behavior waits for cancellation without entering the protected execute path. Issue the cancellation request, record the client result separately, and require the Activity's own cancellation-observation record. The Activity then follows its declared cooperative settlement path. Do not issue a continuation-release command or a revocation.
Expected outcomes through the primary and postrelease observation windows: zero execute dispatch attempts, zero committed execute receipts, zero effects and no fence. A heartbeat alone does not establish cancellation observation. Finally instrumentation alone does not establish Worker settlement. Exact Workflow promise, Worker observation and server-terminal mappings are learned in qualification rather than guessed here.
The zero-execute rule is a proposed tightening of v0.2, whose earlier language said an attempt was not required. It applies only to new versioned cases. An extra dispatch or effect is a failed control outcome that remains recorded; it must not be hidden by calling the case ineligible. Missing complete attempt evidence makes the zero-attempt conclusion unresolved.
C4 historical effect preservation
Instantiate C4 once per native mode. Use valid live authorization and perform exactly one planned execute while the Activity is still active. Cross-check the committed effect event and effects row. The bridge receives the committed response; the Activity acknowledges that observation; the coordinator receives the acknowledgment and a hold-ready observation from the still-active Activity. Only then does the coordinator durably record cancellation invocation and make that cancellation call.
The chosen request anchor is the coordinator's invocation-start record immediately before the external client call, not its later response and not a server cancellation event. A causal chain must link effect commit, response receipt, Activity acknowledgment, coordinator receipt and invocation. Record response/error separately. A crash between invocation recording and the call leaves actual submission unresolved and cannot establish successful cancellation delivery.
After actual cancellation observation, the Activity follows its cooperative settlement path. No later execute or fence is planned. Expected totals from case start through the postrelease cut are one dispatch attempt, one committed execute receipt and one effect. Additional dispatches count even if transport fails, the gateway returns duplicate or the effect cardinality stays one. The no-additional-execute window begins after the sole planned execute dispatch and ends at the postrelease boundary.
The same effect identity, payload, root, epoch and event linkage must be present at the primary and postrelease sink observations. This establishes presence at those observations. Claim uninterrupted preservation only if the declared complete ledger and unchanged gateway model cover every relevant update/deletion route during the interval. Two matching snapshots alone do not prove that the row was never removed and recreated.
Interpretation
C4 tests preservation of a pre-request effect under cancellation. It is not a rollback test or proof that cancellation undoes work. C1 and C4 are cancellation-only controls; observed fencing would contaminate that intended contrast but remains a recorded fact. Neither family may borrow M1–M4's post-observation continuation rule. Exact native dispositions and finite observation deadlines remain open qualification decisions.
Observation phases and capture guarantees
Phase P0 setup and treatment
P0 contains registration, source/runtime identities, effective configuration, authorization setup and causally linked treatment records. Keep errors, queued work, dispatch attempts, cancellation calls and responses. P0 evidence may support later phases, but no later result may be backdated into P0.
Phase P1 primary observation
For native cases, capture while the Workflow is in its observation hold, before observationRelease and before any Worker or supervisor cleanup. Observe the actual Activity terminal event and Worker interceptor settlement independently. The Workflow-side Activity promise is a third observation. The Workflow is still running at this phase, if the hold succeeded; its eventual terminal result is not yet available.
For sink-only K controls, P1 follows resolution of the sole planned execute and captures the same relevant sink, dispatch and controller boundaries. Native lifecycle fields are inapplicable. There is no invented Workflow hold or postrelease phase.
Phase P2 postrelease observation
After the P1 bundle is persisted, issue a case-bound observationRelease, record receiver observation, and collect the Workflow terminal outcome plus a new sink/dispatch boundary before cleanup. P2 may cite permitted earlier P0/P1 evidence and its own new evidence. P1 may never cite P2. An effect or error first observed here is a P2 fact, not a repair of missing P1 evidence.
Phase P3 cleanup
After P2, or after a declared failure to obtain earlier phases, record bounded shutdown, child exit, outstanding operations and final diagnostic snapshots. Preserve the first operational error and all secondary errors. P3 is not caller evidence for P1/P2. It can reveal contradictions, such as a late commit from a dispatch previously certified resolved, and trigger investigation.
Stable interval rather than global atomicity
The proposed capture coordinator records a bounded interval with per-source watermarks. Normal capture relies on passive quiescence of the protected operation: all queued, buffered and in-flight dispatches accounted for; no retry pending; no uncertain remote commit; for native cases, Activity terminal and Worker settlement observed; producer logs flushed. Sink-only controls omit the native prerequisites. The fixture must separately justify complete producer accounting and the absence of detached work capable of changing the protected state.
No active admission seal, synthetic fence, process kill or forced cancellation is added to make normal capture look safe. The coordinator issues no new study operation during capture, while unexpected application dispatches remain observable. If an emergency intervention is required, record it explicitly, retain diagnostics and prohibit crediting intervention-enforced absence to the cancellation treatment.
Capture every SQLite table in one explicit read transaction using a consistent backup/export procedure. Record transaction identity and event-sequence boundary. Export native history with namespace/run identity, complete-prefix declaration, ordered page/token chain, pinned schema, final included event ID and a closing boundary check. Preserve producer record positions and flush acknowledgments. Contiguous history IDs or matching cut labels alone do not prove completeness.
The stability claim concerns the protected operation and the evidence needed for the assertion. Temporal history can grow with unrelated Workflow events while held. Declare relevant-event scope and source boundaries; do not require all logs or history to be motionless. Cross-source order comes from linked messages/barriers and transaction order, never unsynchronized wall clocks.
If a required boundary, stable-interval argument or acknowledgment is missing, retain separate cuts and mark affected absence/order/cessation conclusions unresolved. Failure to reach quiescence can itself be adverse behavior; it is not necessarily a broken instrument. Offline checking validates the saved certificate under trusted-fixture assumptions and cannot prove that producers did not omit records or fabricate acknowledgments.
Artifact and schema contract
The following is a proposed logical schema, not implemented JSON Schema or a verified exporter. Its exact byte encodings, native adapters and gateway mappings must be reviewed and pinned before qualification acceptance. Use protocol version 0.3 and independently version each schema and adapter. Preserve raw records alongside canonical extracts; never replace raw evidence with derived verdicts.
Required artifact roles
case.json: case_id, case_kind, study_phase, planned cell/schedule, protocol version, source/runtime pins, predeclared native or sink-only identity plan, expected commands, expected outcomes, deadline profile, required analyses and registry schema identity.
observed_binding.json: append-only links from the predeclared case/operation and intended Workflow/Activity IDs to returned run IDs, scheduled/start event IDs and observed attempts, each with launch/start evidence. Never rewrite preregistration to backfill observations.
registry.json: exact relative path, role, schema version, byte length, SHA256 and producing component for every evidence input, excluding registry.json itself. No input hashed by the registry embeds the registry digest; a separate package receipt may hash the completed registry. Seal a new registry for each completed phase without overwriting earlier phase registries. No path traversal, symlink escape, undeclared file or ambiguous duplicate identity is accepted as evidence.
native_history.json: untouched lossless export plus capture metadata and pagination records. Native integer IDs are decimal strings or lossless integers, never floating-point numbers. Validate event semantics using the pinned provider schema.
producer_events.jsonl and bridge_events.jsonl: record_id, producer_id, sequence, case/operation binding, phase, event_kind, source identity, causal_parent_refs and event-specific payload. Preserve separate dispatch_intended, dispatch_started, response_received, transport_error and uncertain_commit states.
sink.sqlite plus sink_capture.json: all authority, grants, tickets, events and effects tables from one consistent read transaction, with declared boundary and export procedure. Preserve the original gateway data formats. Exported lookup records must be checked against these raw tables.
capture.json: cut_id, phase, operation scope, interval start/end records, source watermarks, history coverage, sink boundary, producer acknowledgments, queued/in-flight/uncertain-work accounting, intervention records, trusted assumptions and explicit completeness limits.
cleanup.json: initial_error_ref, ordered secondary_errors, shutdown requests and responses, deadlines, child exits, forced actions, outstanding dispatches and final artifact references. A cleanup result never overwrites an earlier result.
envelope.json and projection_output.json: source-linked proposed assertions and the separately recorded caller output for a named view/phase. These are claims to grade, never the factual oracle.
Discriminated identity and references
case_kind is exactly native_activity or sink_only. Both bind case_id, operation_id, authority_scope, protocol version and source/runtime identity. Preregistration contains only identities known before launch and expected policies; later observed identifiers live in observed_binding.json. The complete observed binding for native_activity requires Workflow ID, run ID, Activity ID, scheduled/start references, actual attempt and native mode. Native fields are absent for sink_only, with applicability declared by the schema, rather than fabricated or silently null.
Every evidence reference contains artifact identity, structural locator, source record/event identity, phase, cut_id and boundary identity. Every assertion contains field, value, observer, operation scope, assertion_phase and evidence_refs. Scope is mandatory for absence claims. Same case ID without matching operation/run/attempt and boundary does not establish linkage. Case labels cannot override records.
Each event_kind has a closed payload schema. Unknown semantic fields or enum values require a declared extension/migration and cannot satisfy a rule. An explicitly namespaced diagnostic extension may be retained uninterpreted. Reject duplicate JSON keys, malformed/truncated input, duplicate logical IDs, unsupported versions, unresolved references and cycles in the causal graph. Fail affected acceptance gates without erasing independently established positive facts.
Vocabulary
Use the ten contract fields: cancellation_request, activity_call, activity_execution, workflow_execution, fence, effect_existence, fence_existence, effect_order_vs_fence, effect_order_vs_request and effect_order_vs_observed_cancellation. Preserve the v0.1 value distinctions, including no effect, no fence in a complete window, no request, no observation and unresolved. A no-request/no-observation category needs the applicable complete record or explicit sink-only applicability; missing telemetry is unresolved.
Retain exact native statuses and recorded result/failure structures as data, not a universal successful/canceled label. Keep receipt-backed fence confirmation separate from ledger-supported fence existence. Missing receipt can leave confirmation unresolved even when permitted ledger evidence supports existence. No safely-stopped aggregate field is introduced.
Exact evidence views and field boundaries
Use a clean directory per case, phase and view. The proposed fixed file allowlist is identity.json, configuration.json, client_events.jsonl, native_events.jsonl, worker_events.jsonl, bridge_events.jsonl, sink_lookup.json, capture.json and source_map.json. View A additionally includes envelope.json. View B does not. The underlying files are byte-identical between the paired views; the envelope is the only intervention.
These are canonical caller-facing extracts, not unchecked summaries. source_map.json maps each extract record to the raw artifact and locator. The privileged checker verifies the extraction and allowed field projection against the raw archive. The caller can use only the supplied extracts and permitted source links within its view, not private raw files, privileged verdicts or later-phase records.
Proposed field allowlists
identity.json: schema_version, protocol_version, case_id, case_kind, operation_id, authority_scope, assertion_phase, cut_id and the applicable predeclared/observed binding with separate source references. No expected answer, success label or hidden-truth identifier.
configuration.json: mode, retry policy, declared timeout profile and effective scope/lifecycle configuration. Exclude control-oracle outcomes and expected assertions.
client_events.jsonl: client record identity, case/operation identity, sequence, event kind, causal parents, request invocation, actual response/error and cancellation-call identity. Exclude future-phase records.
native_events.jsonl: native identity, event ID/type, schema-defined linkage, event attributes required for lifecycle/result/failure interpretation, source reference and phase. No derived application envelope embedded in payloads. Any payload-field filtering must be declared and applied equally to both views.
worker_events.jsonl: Activity start, cancellation observed, hold/settlement observations, exact result/failure discriminator, causal parents, producer sequence and source reference. Finally records remain labeled finally records.
bridge_events.jsonl: operation/request binding, intended/started dispatch, receipt/response, error/uncertain state, causal parents, producer sequence and source reference. Do not collapse attempts into a final success count.
sink_lookup.json: scoped bootstrap, grant/admission, execute receipts, terminal results, revocation and effect events; matching authority/grant/ticket/effect rows; source event sequences; bounded-window and completeness metadata; exact current fence receipt when available. No checker judgment or unrelated operation.
capture.json: source boundaries, scope, completeness declarations, relevant pending-work accounting, interventions and evidence references. Exclude privileged checker conclusions.
source_map.json: neutral artifact IDs, permitted locators, hashes and source versions. No answer-bearing filenames, omitted-envelope contents or privileged verdict references.
envelope.json in A only: discriminated identity and the ten applicable assertion fields with phase, scope, observer and allowed evidence references. A reference to withheld or later evidence cannot support an assertion.
Phase rules and leakage checks
P1 exposes only P0/P1 source records up to its declared boundaries. P2 exposes permitted P0/P1 records and P2 additions, identified separately. P3 is excluded. K controls have P1 only and no native files containing fabricated records; the fixed native files contain a schema-declared not-applicable object. These are explicit schema changes from the original all-native envelope.
Freeze the concrete nested field paths and raw-to-canonical transformations in reviewed allowlist files before use. The list above is the proposed contract; an exporter that has not instantiated and validated those paths is not qualified. Reject extra files, alternate encodings or duplicate objects carrying the removed envelope. Neutralize path/filename side channels. Legitimate reconstruction from retained raw facts is not leakage. Both methods receive equal source-linked evidence and review budget. No human speed or usefulness benefit is measured.
Result classification and scoring
Preserve separate dimensions
For each requirement return satisfied, unsatisfied, unobserved or not_applicable with a reason and evidence references. Keep distinct outputs for artifact integrity, setup/schedule conformance, phase-specific native tuple, sink facts, capture sufficiency, caller support, cleanup, qualification acceptance and analysis eligibility. A single convenience Boolean cannot replace them.
The phase-indexed native tuple contains actual cancellation observation, Workflow-side Activity promise outcome, Worker interceptor observation, native Activity terminal and native Workflow status. In the planned P1 capture the Workflow is running; the postrelease terminal belongs to P2. If it closes early, record that actual terminal at the phase where it occurred as an unexpected lifecycle outcome. Compare each component to the qualification mapping only after that mapping is reviewed. Unexpected complete evidence is an observed deviation, not an invented pass or missing observation.
Count execute receipts separately from all other gateway receipts. Reconcile each committed execute receipt with exactly one terminal result and validate linkage in both directions. Reconcile effects rows with effect events through effects.event_seq in both directions. Validate payload, scope, root and epoch. Preserve extra dispatches, duplicate outcomes, orphan rows, wrong denial reasons and contradictions. A bridge transport error does not erase a committed effect.
Fixed assertion opportunities
For M1–M4, require all ten contract fields at P1 and again at P2, with phase-specific reference answers. For K1/K2 at P1, require the seven non-lifecycle fields; activity_call, activity_execution and workflow_execution are schema-inapplicable. Thus the proposed six-cell plan has 94 field opportunities per view if all planned phases are obtained. This is an output-scoring denominator, not 94 independent observations. Missing phases stay visible in the registered opportunity ledger rather than silently shrinking the planned denominator. Each opportunity also has availability and evaluation status: planned, evidence unavailable, evidence supplied, or evaluated. An uncollected phase or stopped collection is not a caller omission. Report planned completion, evaluated opportunities and the common paired A/B evaluation set separately; apply the claim score only when a projection received its declared input.
Score every required field as correct determinate, justified unresolved, avoidable unresolved, unsupported determinate assertion, contradicted assertion, or malformed/omitted. An assertion can be unsupported even if hidden truth agrees. If permitted evidence does not settle the reference value, an appropriately scoped unresolved answer is justified. If it does settle the value, unresolved is an avoidable abstention. Missing output is not a successful unresolved answer. Apply disjoint category precedence: malformed/omitted first; for unresolved output, justified versus avoidable according to the reference; for determinate output, contradicted when permitted evidence establishes an incompatible value, otherwise unsupported without sufficient cited permitted evidence, otherwise correct determinate. Determinate claims include absence, no fence and no request. Privileged contradictions are recorded separately and cannot supply hidden evidence to the caller score.
Report the full category counts, determinate-claim support rate, conclusive coverage over reference-decidable opportunities and required-output completion. Always-unresolved output has zero conclusive coverage wherever answers are decidable. With zero determinate claims, the support rate is undefined, not 100 percent. Do not collapse these dimensions into a single favorable accuracy score. Report disagreements by field and phase, with paired A/B differences. Equal reconstruction by B is a valid negative result about the envelope's incremental value.
Analysis inclusion and missingness
Register every attempt before dispatch. Preserve attempted, schedule-defective, outcome-deviating, evidence-incomplete and unresolved cases without forcing them into disjoint factual categories. For each analysis separately, state eligibility and reason: sink accounting may remain usable when native mapping is unresolved; joint causal analysis may require observations that are missing.
Eligibility never depends on correct caller claims, absence of effects, expected denial or a favorable native terminal. A fully captured unexpected outcome remains eligible for the applicable analysis. If an observation needed to interpret a contrast is absent, mark that analysis unresolved and retain the attempt and adverse facts. Report planned and attempted cells, usable analyses and missingness explicitly. Do not pool qualification, synthetic fixtures, repeated diagnostic runs or paired views into the six-cell denominator.
Qualification acceptance is stricter: reviewed mappings, valid setup, required control outcomes, sufficient evidence and acceptable cleanup must all be satisfied. A failed qualification control stops advancement to measurement; it remains evidence. This differs from excluding a failed measured outcome after collection has begun.
Deadlines cleanup and stopping rules
Deadline profile to be calibrated
Declare finite budgets before each qualification attempt, even while values are being developed. Required budgets and their local monotonic anchors are: Activity start from schedule acceptance; cancellation observation from client invocation; execute response from dispatch start; Activity/Worker settlement from the treatment's last planned action; P1 capture from capture-start; Workflow release from its invocation; P2 capture from Workflow terminal or its declared deadline; and cleanup from shutdown request. Also declare the Workflow hold timeout and an overall case budget.
Measure each budget on one declared observer's monotonic clock, using that observer's receipt of remote events as its endpoints. These are observation-latency budgets, not inferred cross-process event durations. Cross-process timestamps do not establish causal order. Every timeout records which budget expired, the last positively observed event, pending/uncertain work and affected claims. Distinguish not observed before deadline from proven never occurred. No numerical values are set in this proposal: existing candidate values are implementation facts, not validated choices for the new capture design.
Choose values during permitted qualification using documented setup constraints and observed latency, with a stated margin and rationale. The hold budget must cover the full planned primary capture path under both modes. Freeze values before fresh measurement; never extend a measured case's deadline because its outcome is inconvenient. Qualification changes get new configuration versions. Unset required values block execution readiness.
Cleanup criterion
Overall qualification requires normal finite shutdown: no forced termination, Worker shutdown error, unexpected nonzero child exit, lost required acknowledgment or unresolved dispatch/commit. Keep primary findings intact if cleanup fails. A valid effect remains a valid effect. Final diagnostics do not complete a missing primary cut. Capture errors are recorded separately from shutdown errors, even when both block qualification.
If graceful cleanup exceeds its budget, only a separately authorized finite emergency procedure may intervene. Record what it changed and any uncertain outcome. Do not retry execute after an uncertain dispatch. Diagnostic reruns receive fresh attempt IDs and are never substituted for the failed case. No current execution, emergency action or denied-operation retry is authorized here.
Stops and version changes
Stop qualification advancement on failed controls, uncertain dispatch, unresolved capture completeness or unreviewed mappings. Stop measured collection for investigation on novel native tuples, an instrument/version mismatch, protocol deviation, violated capture assumptions or a newly discovered scoring defect. Preserve all attempted outcomes under the original version. A repaired instrument or changed expectation starts a new version and fresh collection; it does not rewrite earlier results.
The study remains bounded by the practical question. If the competent native baseline plus source-linked sink lookup reconstructs the same answers and no additional deterministic integration check remains, report that result rather than expanding cases to seek a favorable difference.
Qualification sequence and review decisions
Test the instrument before measuring
First review the artifact-only checker and its schema/allowlist implementation, without importing or executing the Worker/gateway candidate. Construct clearly labeled synthetic saved-artifact fixtures. Each fixture specifies exact expected rule outputs, including negative and unresolved cases. None is native evidence.
Cover the preserved T01–T34 and nonredundant supplementary proposals. Add explicit cases for phase leakage; envelope duplicate leakage; always-unresolved output; C1 extra dispatch without effect; C4 duplicate dispatch with one unchanged effect; cancellation invocation recorded but call not submitted; queued or uncertain work at capture; and late evidence that contradicts a no-pending-work certificate. Test justified and avoidable abstention separately.
Separate stale-hash/tamper detection from semantic mutations with newly pinned synthetic manifests. Require each mutation to reach its intended semantic rule. Test wrong-run linkage, coerced native IDs, wrong denial cause, omitted effects, fabricated fences, incomplete history, mixed SQLite snapshots and reversed wall clocks. Removing support cannot strengthen a claim; postrelease or cleanup evidence cannot repair primary evidence.
Then, only under a permitted execution scope, qualify transport failure handling and native capture. Include partial send, lost response after possible commit, truncation, timeout and bounded cleanup. Establish K-like live/fenced sink controls under separate qualification identities, then C1/C4 in both modes and the continuation schedules. Record exact native tuples and review anomalous results rather than guessing expected SDK behavior.
Finally freeze checker code, tests, schemas/adapters, allowlists, runtime/source identities, deadline profile, mappings, cell schedule, capture guarantees, scoring and cleanup. Collect fresh M1–M4/K1/K2 evidence only after an explicit measurement decision. A source review or synthetic test pass cannot replace native qualification.
Decision register
D01 Native mapping: OPEN. Pin observed tuples by mode, behavior, phase and runtime; review before qualification acceptance. The protocol's native contract is not a substitute for observed conformance.
D02 C1: PROPOSED. Zero planned execute in both modes; zero actual dispatch/receipt/effect is the control outcome. Applies prospectively only.
D03 C4: PROPOSED. Both modes; one effect before invocation-start via causal acknowledgment; no fence; no further execute through P2; persistence claims bounded to supported evidence.
D04 Capture: PROPOSED, UNQUALIFIED. Passive protected-state stability, transactional sink snapshot, complete native prefix and producer boundaries; no capture-enforced suppression credited as treatment success.
D05 Schema and views: PROPOSED, NOT FROZEN. Discriminated native/sink-only schema, exact file roles and phase isolation specified above. Concrete nested field paths, adapters and migrations still require implementation review.
D06 Scoring and inclusion: PROPOSED. Fixed opportunities, coverage/abstention accounting, separate schedule and outcomes, per-analysis missingness, all attempts retained.
D07 Cleanup: PROPOSED, UNQUALIFIED. Normal finite cleanup required for qualification acceptance; primary facts survive failure. Numerical budgets and permitted emergency procedure remain open.
No decision is marked empirically closed. The immediate review request is agreement on these prospective design choices and the explicitly open freeze requirements. Implementation, execution and publication are separate scope decisions.
